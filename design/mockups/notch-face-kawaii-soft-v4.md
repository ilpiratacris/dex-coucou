## Prompt v4 integrale

Refine the attached notch companion concept board into a more tactile, soft, fluid, rubbery yet extremely minimal contemporary premium UI design. Preserve the Japanese kawaii personality: simple tiny dot/rounded blob eyes, expressive sparse curved mouth, NO body, nose, ears, avatar badge or robot head. The entire black rounded desktop notch IS the close-up face. We want Apple-like refined restraint and precise soft curves, with the gently elastic tactile feel of silicone, not rigid flat vector lines. All visible facial features should be soft pale mint-white organic forms with slight rounded volume and a very delicate diffuse pearly lighting, almost imperceptible shading, not metallic or glossy. Eyes like tiny soft silicone beads, sleepy eyes like squashable rounded dashes, smiling mouth like a very thin soft flexible silicone arc. The black notch surface should be deep charcoal smooth with generous organic continuous corner curvature, barely perceptible soft ambient edge shading. Emotions are achieved by subtle squashing, stretching and curved soft deformation, not adding decoration. Warm, quiet, small charming presence, a face that looks gently squishy and alive. No bright neon, no LED pixels, no gradients in bright rainbow, no excessive glass, no cyberpunk, no photoreal toy placed in notch. Generate a landscape refined design board on warm off-white background. TOP: large clear UI examples of COMPACT notch with only two small soft eyes, and EXPANDED wider taller notch with two soft eyes and smile. BOTTOM: six consistent notch expressions in a tidy 3 by 2 grid: calm smile, sleepy with short closed eyes, curious eyes looking sideways and small softly open round mouth, concentrated with gently compressed eye shapes rather than hard angry eyebrows, happy with curved smiling eyes and small rounded open smile, playful wink. Facial forms vary with gentle elastic asymmetry; never rigid sharp angles. Under each small example use only small clean Italian labels: 'Sereno', 'Assonnato', 'Curioso', 'Concentrato', 'Felice', 'Complice'. Top labels 'Compatto' and 'Espanso'. Add a small detail strip at the very bottom with three close-ups of the same eye gently deforming from round to squashed to round, suggesting an elastic animation keyframe sequence; label it 'Movimento morbido'. Use quiet misty mint desktop wallpaper behind each notch, NO physical monitor hardware or bezel, no extraneous marketing copy. Carefully preserve black negative space and small facial elements, calm and sophisticated visual identity, high fidelity mockup.

## Indicazioni per la futura animazione

Proposte di design, da valutare nella successiva implementazione:

- Sguardo che segue il mouse con un leggero ritardo e assestamento dolce.
- Occhi che si schiacciano e si riallungano durante il battito.
- Bocca che cambia forma in modo continuo fra espressioni.
- Espansione del notch con curva elastica e rimbalzo appena percepibile.
- Variante a movimento ridotto, evitando oscillazioni persistenti.

La tavola statica suggerisce il materiale e le deformazioni; non dimostra la fluidità di un'animazione né la resa nella finestra nativa Windows.
